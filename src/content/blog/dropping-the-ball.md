I missed the mark... I didn't invite anyone from work to our church sponsored barbeque and I didn't invite my neighbors.  I really fell short.  I have excusses, but why bother?  I missed it, plain and simple.  What do I need to do to change?  It's too simplistic to say, "<em>I can't change myself, I have to rely on God</em>" but too overwelming to say "<em>I'll pull myself up by my bootstraps, and fix this thing</em>".  It has to be somewhere in the middle.
