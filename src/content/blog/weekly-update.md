<p class="MsoNormal" style="margin: 0in 0in 0pt">I've updated the weekly update section.  I hate to know that I'm three weeks behind.  I gained several pounds during those three weeks, but when I weighted yesterday, I was only half a pound up from the last time I officially weighted in.  It is time to stop looking back and start looking forward.  I have three weeks before my reunion.  If I maintain my two pound a week weight loss goal, I will be 284 lbs.  That's not the 274 lbs. I had hoped for but its better than the 330 lbs I was at my heaviest.  I haven't been riding my bike much lately, but I really feel that my biggest reason for not losing weight is my diet.  I keep making goals to write down everything that I eat, but I have continued to fall short here.  I haven't done a bod pod test in a while either.  The main reason I suppose is that I haven't been looking forward to the results.  I will get one next week.  I must not stop looking at my weight goals, my diet goals, my exercise goals because I missed the mark - if I do, then I will stop trying to hit the mark.  I will push forward, I will continue to push myself, I will lose weight... </p>
