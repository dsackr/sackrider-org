When King David recaptured the Arch of the Covenent, he had it loaded onto a cart pulled by oxen.  At some point the cart began to tip over a [name] grabbed the Arch to steady it.  The Bible records that he was instantly killed.

The Arch of the Covenent represented the presence of God.  Our Bible tells us that God inhabits the praises of his people.

In the gospel of [name] chapter [number], Jesus says "if they do not cry out, these stones will"

Genesis speaks of Nimrod's reign over Bebel, telling us they had 'bricks' for stone.  Bricks are man made, but stones are made only by God.  Nimrod setup the first government known to man in which he directed the people to replace that which God made, with that which was made by man.

David attempted to have God's Presence carried on a cart, but you cannot praise God through a proxy.  Often times we sit in Church and watch the 'worship' leader sing, listening to the 'band' and call that a praise and worship service.  God's desire is not to be worshiped through mechanizm or system - but out of a personal overwelming sense of adoration and insperation.

When King David had the Arch carried by man as required by law, he took off his garments began to dance before the LORD.
