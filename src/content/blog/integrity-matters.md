Our church's married couple's ministry recently (Friday night) hosted a dinner theater called "Murder and a Meal".  It promised to be great fun for us all.  Maybe the Married Couple Ministry Team should have previewed the show before we booked it; maybe they should have asked for a script - they did speak to the producer about the show.  They did ask about the rating of the show.  They were told it was normally 'PG-13' but that it could be tamed to a 'PG' rating.  Ratings have changed... 

Before I continue on the subject of integrity, I have a sidenote: I know the following story could sound like a bunch of Christians got offended at a few dirty jokes.  I even did some introspection following the evening regarding just that concept.  I will address more on this issue at the end of the post...
The night started out with everyone showing up to the hotel, ready to have a great time.  We each were given name tags with clever names like "Justin Case" and "Hidi Valuables".  We were seated and awaited the night's festivities to begin.  Scene one was filled with brief sexual innuendos and language.  The environment was getting a little tense as many of the couples in the group were shocked at the risqué content of the play.
One of the leadership team members discretely left the room to speak with the director backstage about the concerns shared among most of the couples.  Believing scene two was sure to exclude the offensive content; the leadership team again looked forward to the evening.  After increasing innuendo, and foul language in scene two, the leadership team made a tough choice.  They stopped the show.
I've been told that integrity is doing what's right when no one is looking.  I believe integrity is also doing what's right when everyone's looking.  It would have been easy to sit through the rest of the show and simply apologize to those who were offended
