This Sunday night starting at 6:00 pm, we are hosting one of the church's Summer Backyard Barbeque!  Yes, you are invited.  I'm also inviting my neighbors on all sides.  I want to really begin to reach out to others and show the love of Christ.  I just realized that I didn't invite anyone from work, and that makes me sad.  I think I'm going to call a couple of them tomorrow and invite them even though its a little late now.  Maybe they will come.

Stephanie and I have a lot of work to do.  We live like slobs (I know, we <em>should</em> start working on that, but one thing at a time -see My Weightloss-, OK?).  The house needs to be cleaned up, the weeds in both yards need to be pulled, the patio needs to be cleaned up, the pool needs to be cleaned out, etc., etc., etc...  Anyway, I'm hoping we can get it done quickly because I want to spend part of the day enjoying the day.
