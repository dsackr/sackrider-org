I don't usually post on such grave matters, however I just read several online posting about gun control in response to the recent horrible event at Virgina Tech.

One such post had this quote:
<blockquote><em>When will we learn that freedom and guns do not play well together?</em></blockquote>
The students at Virgina Tech were killed by a deranged lunatic. Jack the Ripper used knives, Hitler used gas chambers, Jim Jones used poison, Cho used a gun - Guns are not evil, Cho was.

If the students were allowed to carry guns on campus maybe this could have been stopped before 32 people were killed -- remember the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachian_School_of_Law_shooting" title="Appalachian" target="_blank">Appalachian school shooting</a> in 2002? Two students went to their cars, got their guns, and stopped a killer just like this one.

Criminalize guns - and only the criminals will have guns.
