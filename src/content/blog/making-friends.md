A few disconnected thoughts regarding friendships in my life...

I haven't had a lot of close personal friends in my life. It wasn't until High School that I found myself with close guy friends at all. In junior high school I met Emily Troll. I met her on the bus my eighth grade year after I had transferred from another school. She was kind and we became friends because she reached out to me. The problem with friends of the opposite sex is that I would find myself becoming infatuated with them and eventually seek more than just friendship. That would end up ruining the friendship. I did have a crush on Emily but never acted on it, we simply grew apart as we got into high school.

I don't think I really knew how to make friends. I used to listen to the guys in the football locker room talk with their friends from elementary school and wish I had friends like that. Most all of my relationships were superficial. I was a funny and annoying class clown that sometimes was fun to be around, but never the good friend people turned to. It wasn't until Shannon and Chip that I had any guy friends. I met Shannon at school but it was at church that our friendship grew. Chip and Shannon were friends so I also got to know Chip, also at church.

When I left for college, I moved 500 miles away from home and I left my only two guy friendships behind. At bible college, I met Richard and Andy - both lived in the dorms. Richard and I were roommates so a friendship came naturally there (I guess it just as easily could have gone the other way, but we got along great). Richard was friends with Andy, so I alsogot to know Andy. The three of us became somewhat famous at bible college as "the drama dudes".

Sidebar: It seems whereever I go, drama follows. I write drama skits - started at my small church in North Carolina with a guy named Alan. He was friends with Toyna, a young woman at our church. Alan was only there for a week or so, but his influence pushed me from just being a class clown to being a drama guy.

Anyway, Andy and Richard and I were asked to perform on many occasions, several were in the youth group and a few times in the main sactuary that seats about 5200 people. But we all graduated and Andy went home to Washington State, while Richard moved to Florida to attend another college.

By this time I was running the drama ministry at the youth group. I met many great people but only developed a few close friends. Chris was a member of my team that I later roomed with. He was my roommate right up until I got married to my awesome wife Stephanie.

Jason was another member of the drama team that I became friends with. I often found two guys (often they would already be friends) to hang with and do drama with, but to be honest very very few of those friendships turned into deep meaningful relationships.

I have only had one 'best friend' in my whole life (my wife not withstanding). Any that friendship only developed to that level after years of what I call forced interaction. Jason and I were kinda friends when he was in drama. Even after he got married and Chris got married and I got married and we all hung out as couple friends, we hadn't yet reached the Godly accountablity brotherhood friendship that it is today.

Jason and I worked at the same job, went to the same church, hung out with the same friends but it was only after years of car pooling two hours a day, monday thru friday - getting in trouble at work together, getting fustrated with our plights as ministers working secular jobs, arguing, ticking each other off, disagreeing on the bible, disagreeing about work, disagreeing about church, asking each other for accountablity and really putting pressure on each other when we aren't where we know we should be and through it all, we got back in the same car the next morning and rode to work.
