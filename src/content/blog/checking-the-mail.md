Do you remember checking the mail as a kid?  I used to look forward to getting a letter in the mail from a family member or a friend.  Then as I got older, moved out of my parents home, and started living on my own, I got my own bills along with letters.  I also got junk mail , advertisements, and "Have you seen me" postcards.  I stopped looking forward to checking the mail when I realized that more often than not I not going to see em
