Finding friends almost feels like dating used to feel when I was single.  Think about it - when you are dating, you look for someone you might click with, someone you would enjoy spending time with, right?  Then you ask them out - maybe in a group setting or to coffee.  If things go well, you might ask them to dinner.  Over dinner, you spend a lot of time talking about everything you can thing of.  During the whole process you are concerned about rejection and acceptance.  Sometimes you even let the fear of rejection keep you from pursuing a relationship.  As Stephanie and I meet new couples at the church, we look to see if we click.  We might ask them if they want grab something to eat after church and if everything is going well, we might ask them over to dinner.  And like dating, we sometimes wonder if other couples will like us - if they will accept or reject us.<!--more-->
