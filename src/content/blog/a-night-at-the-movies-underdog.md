Family night out tonight with Zander was fun.  We went to see Underdog because it was the only movie playing (other than Hairspray) that was rated PG.  The main character is a police dog that isn't very good at his job (voice by [Jason Lee](http://imdb.com/name/nm0005134/) - The guy from "My Name is Earl").  Through a series of events, the dog (later named Shoeshine) is removed from his job, captured by a lab, experimented on by a mad scientist (played by [Peter Dinklage](http://imdb.com/name/nm0227759/)), and finally finds himself in a home with a widower (played by [James Belushi](http://imdb.com/name/nm0000902/)) and the widower's son.
