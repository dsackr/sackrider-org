![Car Thief](/wp-content/uploads/2008/01/carthief.jpg)Last Saturday, my wife and I arranged to have Grammie Sackrider come over to watch the kids while we met another couple from the church to go hiking.  When we went to get into the car, I noticed the console was opened up and all the trash (we use it as a trash can in the car) was pulled out and placed on my seat.  My first thought was that Stephanie must have been looking for something, but when she said it wasn't her, I quickly realized what had happened.  My car was broken into!
