Like a lot of you, I watched Saturday morning cartoons as a kid. One of my favorites growing up was Ghostbusters, based on the popular 1984 movie by the same title. In one particular episode, all of New York was the stage for a thousand year old battle between good and evil. In an effort to save New York (and the world) from the carnage, the Ghostbusters suggest a baseball game decide the battle instead of an all out war; both sides agree.

During the game, the Evil side was cheating and the Ghostbusters lose faith that Good can prevail. They suggest cheating to even the playing field but the neural umpire says "If you cheat, you become evil - and evil wins. They are allowed to cheat because they are evil. You are not, because you are good."

I never forgot that scene. If you do evil things, you are evil. It seems pretty simple to a child. I understood that it wasn't fair that evil gets away with cheating while good cannot, but I also believed good had to win without cheating if good was going to win at all.
