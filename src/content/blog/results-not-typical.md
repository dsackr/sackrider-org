![Results not typical](/wp-content/uploads/2008/01/resultnottypical.jpg)I just saw a commercial for one of the many weight loss systems available in the market today.  This one will ship the food right to your door and shows several 'actual customers' talking about their 50, 60, even 100 lbs weight loss.  But there on the screen are those three little words - ***Results not typical***.  My favorite was 'Results atypical'.  Atypical?  My eight grade English teacher taught me that he was a theist - meaning he believed in a god, as opposed to an atheist which does not.  Atypical means not typical - very clever, but I digress.
