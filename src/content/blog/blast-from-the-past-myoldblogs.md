Oh, by the way, some of the posts were writen when I was feeling a little hopeless due to the long cold winter weather and my not being in ministry full time but remember these are over a year old, so please don't attempt to cheer me up based on what you read in <a title="myOldBlogs" href="http://sackrider.org/myblog/category/category/myoldblogs/">myOldBlogs</a>.
