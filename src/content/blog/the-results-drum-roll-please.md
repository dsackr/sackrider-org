My RMR (resting metabolic rate) is 2,208 kcal/day. That means I burn 2,208 calories everyday without doing anything. So if I do nothing today but watch TV, I'm going to burn 2,208 calories. It takes 3,500 to lose a pound of fat. I eat maybe 3,000 - 3,500 calories a day, and I'm guessing I'm burning 4,000-5,000 a day when I ride. At that rate, I should loose 2-3 lbs for a week. If I can cut my diet to 2500 calories a day, I will lose an additional 2 lbs a week. I'm going back in two weeks -- I'll post my results in the "Weekly Update...".
