My wife is a pastor's daughter. She has spent a lot of her life listening to gifted speakers, including her father. Stephanie told me how important it was that I be a gifted speaker as well, but in all our friendship and subsequent dating she had never heard me speak. After we married, she heard me give impromptu speeches but never a full sermon. She's heard me practice my speaking and I've used her as a sounding board many times to work out the rough edges of a poetic line or two, but never a full sermon. When she realized that fact, she blurted out to me "Anyone can preach a soundbite!" What? Anyone can preach a soundbite?
