Have you ever eaten at a buffet? My wife and I recently went out to eat at a Chinese buffet and like many people do at a buffet, I over ate.  I felt so miserable afterward.  I thought about those people that overeat and then force themselves to throwup.  Later that same night, I rented a movie that was 'all the buzz' around the office.  I had heard it was very funny and the reviews were all stars and thumbs up, so I got it even though it's rated R.

I didn't find it funny, but I kept watching.  To be honest, I found most of it offensive with a few short moments of comedy buried here and there.  But - I kept watching.  By the end of the movie I felt bloated but not from the buffet.  I had just stuffed my mind with ideas of drug abuse, one night stands, and foul language.  I went to bed night thinking I wish I could throw up the spiritual junk I had just force feed myself.  That's when I realized...

That's when I realized that moments like this aren't the really bad ones.  Its not the buffet were I ate so much I couldn't walk that got me to weight 300lbs.  Its the everyday habits - the extra roll, the extra butter, that second helping of dessert.  Its the slightly larger than last time portions that put me in the guests of unusal size catagory at theme parks.  And if the everyday is how I got to look like this physically, how bloated must I look in the spirit.
