Have you ever eaten at a buffet? My wife and I recently went out to eat at a Chinese buffet and like many people do at a buffet, I over ate.  I felt so miserable afterward.  I thought about those people that overeat and then force themselves to throwup.  Later that same night, I rented a movie that was 'all the buzz' around the office.  I had heard it was very funny and the reviews were all stars and thumbs up, so I got it even though it's rated R.
