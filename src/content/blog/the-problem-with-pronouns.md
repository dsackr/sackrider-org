A pronoun is a function word this is used in place of a noun. We use them often to simplify John, Sally, and Dale saw the movie together. <em>They </em>agreed <em>it </em>was amazing.

Without pronouns the second statements above would quite a bit different:
<p style="padding-left: 30px;">John, Sally, and Dale saw the movie together. <em>John, Sally, and Dale</em> agreed <em>the movie</em> was amazing.</p>
The first phrasing is easily understood and more concise, however the second phrasing offers a clear understanding of who is being referred to. After reading the pamphlet, Judy threw <strong>it</strong> into the garbage can.
