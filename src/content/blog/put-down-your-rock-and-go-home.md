[![Stoning](/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/Stoning.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/Stoning.jpg)John 8:1-11 tells the story about a woman caught in adultery, taken before Jesus by the religious leaders of the day. They didn't value His teaching, but instead were looking to trap him by making him either agree to stone her according to the law of Moses (kinda goes against that whole love and forgive message He was preaching) OR have him say don't stone her (which goes against the law of Moses).
