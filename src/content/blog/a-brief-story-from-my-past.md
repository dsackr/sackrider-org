<p>Tragic Story with a lesson learned:</p>
<p>Years ago (circa 2002), a guy at our church gave a testimony that he was debt free after paying off several tens of thousands of dollars. I approached him to ask how he did it and he invited my wife and I to his house after church to talk about it.</p>
<p>We sat there late after a Wednesday night service while he ate a potato and told us about the debt snowball and envelope style budgeting. He suggested we talk to another guy from the church that was a financial adviser.</p>
<p>We scheduled that meeting and went over our budget, encouraged to follow the envelope system, follow a "debt snowball" approach, and buy life insurance.</p>
<p>So far, this sounds great... but notice nobody said Dave Ramsey or his financial class, Financial Peace University (FPU).</p>
<p>Continuing with the adviser was around $100 a month, which we did for a few months. We bought life insurance from the adviser which I now understand in Dave Ramsey vernacular was a 'captive agent'. We followed the envelope budget and the debt snowball to pay off our "consumer debt". We were "debt free!" for about a minute and a half...</p>
<p>The whole process was very Dave-"ish". BUT we never learned that debt of any kind was stealing our future. We never connected with DR's teachings directly and the lessons we were taught were only part of the story.</p>
<p>I'm not suggesting DR is somehow special here - only that the lessons of debt, being a slave to the leader, were not emphasized. As a result, we didn't run from the lender like a gazelle from the hunter and we got back into debt over and over again.</p>
<p>THIS TIME, we've taken FPU online, we watch and listen to the radio show, we've read the books, and we bought into the baby step zero: we will never borrow money again!</p>
<p>Its important that if you are going to tell your friends and family about your journey - that you don't do Dave-'ish' testimonies. Emphasis that all debt is the issue, not just credit cards or car loans.</p>
