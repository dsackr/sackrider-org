<p>We were going to do the same last Saturday, but after being out late at the drive-in movies Friday night, we both over slept.  We could have still gone out, but we both wanted an excuse to be lazy.  Anyway, our goal is to be more and more active - one Saturday of staying in is Ok, but with Zander coping everything we do, being active is the example we want to set.
</p>
