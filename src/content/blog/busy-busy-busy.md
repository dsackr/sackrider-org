Ok, here's the deal - if you have been reading my blog so far, you already know that my old job asked that I send back my laptop.  So I brought a new one, it came in last week.  All I still needed to do was copy over my files and pack up the old laptop.  Apparently, my old laptop had some hard drive errors so now I've been having trouble getting my files backed up.  For the most part, it's been useless stuff that I could just delete and move on, however I lost one picture for sure and I may have lost a wav file with my sermon notes on it.  Stephanie brought me a sound recorder a year or so ago for me to record my thoughts to later type out - I never typed these out and I don't want to lose them.  I've been up too late every night trying to get this done and because of that I haven't been able to get out of bed in the morning to ride my bike.  I have to leave now in fact to get to work, and its already 8:15am so I'm having to drive.  There are many other things going on, and I will be able to share several of them (not all :)) in a week or so.  I know that if I stop posting, you stop checking the site for updates... So I wanted to get something up here for you to know that I'm still here. 
<p />
<p style="line-height: 14.4pt">As for my weekly update this week - Monday was my anniversary (7 years) and I didn't weight because I know the results would not have been good after the weekend of no diet and last week of only riding one day on my bike.  I will post more on all of these soon... Thank you to the ones that do check my blog regular, I appreciate you interest in my life and your encouragement in my endeavors.</p>
