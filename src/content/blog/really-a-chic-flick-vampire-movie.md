<tt>Quick Disclaimer: if you are one of my readers that would be appalled at a Harry Potter movie - you may not care for this post. For everyone else, there are no plot spoilers...</tt>

Is nothing sacred any more???  I saw a preview for <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt1099212/" target="_self">Twilight</a> (2008) and thought to myself, "Cool - A new vampire movie is coming out!"  I NEVER would have imaged it to be a CHICK FLICK!  Some of you may not know that I enjoy this particular type of movie, but vampire movies are at the top of my list of "types of movies I enjoy despite my being religious".  Most recently, I enjoyed <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0480249/" target="_blank">I Am Legend</a> (2007) - the book was vampire, the movie, not so much - <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0320691/" target="_blank">Underworld</a> (2003) and <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0401855/" target="_blank">Underworld: Evolution</a> (2006); I've seen all three of the <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0120611/" target="_blank">Blade</a> movies (1998, 2002, 2004) with <a href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000648/" target="_blank">Wesley Snipes</a> although they weren't very good; My all time favorite is <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0093437/" target="_blank">The Lost Boys</a> (1987) - they just don't make classics like that anymore.  I even sat through <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0098577/" target="_blank">Vampire's Kiss</a> (1989) with <a href="http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000115/" target="_blank">Nicolas Cage</a> which was one of the worse movies ever.  <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0110148/" target="_blank">Interview with a Vampire</a>,  <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0118276/" target="_blank">Buffy the Vampire Slayer</a>, <a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0162065/" target="_blank">Angel (TV Series)</a>, I could go on an on, but back to Twilight...

You can image how I would be excited about a new vampire movie.  So, I went to see it.  I was a bit suprised to see so many girls in the theater - I mean, this is a vampire movie, right?  What I didn't know was that the movie is based on a young adult novel by Stephenie Meyers (<a href="http://www.borders.com/online/store/TitleDetail?sku=0316038377" target="_blank">Twilight</a>).  The entire movie was filled with awkward teenage "saved by the bell" moments, which wouldn't have bothered me if it weren't for the constant giggling all around me.  I felt like I was crashing a slumber party with a bunch of school girls (I'm a dad, there is nothing exciting about that idea for me).

As with every vampire movie, the were several twists to the traditional vampire legend - no garlic, I don't remember seeing a single fang, and sunlight didn't have quite the same affect as expected.  They did elude to the tension with werewolves - which is traditionally the Hatfields verses the McCoys for vampires stories, but there were no actual werewolves in the film.  I did have a favorite line in the movie:
<p style="padding-left: 30px;">Chick: I want answers</p>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;">Dude: Yes... No... To get to the other side... 1.77245...</p>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;">Chick: I don't want to know the square root of PI!</p>
<p style="padding-left: 30px;">Dude: You <em>knew </em>that was the square root of pi???</p>

The movie itself, 2 1/2 out of 4 stars, the overall experince, 1 out of 4 stars...
