OK -- I'm not the Proclaimers singing "<em>When I wake up</em>" but I DID RIDE 500 MILES!  My bike had about 150 miles on it when I got here to Phoenix, AZ.  Today, my bike rolled over 500 miles!  I worked from home today, but I wanted some exercise in despite the fact that I didn't have to commute.  So after work, I jumped on my bike and put the 5 miles on it that I needed to hit the 500 mile mark.

My goal is to put 100 miles a week on my bike for as long as I'm working at [my current job].  If I change jobs, then I'll adjust my goal basised on my commute. :)
